About

Filed in Japan turns Japanese company disclosures into short English notes. Most filings by Japanese listed companies are published only in Japanese, on EDINET, the Financial Services Agency's disclosure system. We read the original filings and translate the numbers and facts that matter.

How the notes are made

  • Every figure comes from a public filing (EDINET or the company's own release), and the source is linked.
  • Drafts are prepared with the help of AI translation and automated checks that compare each number with the source data, and are then published.
  • Where the translation and the Japanese original differ, the original filing prevails.

Not investment advice

Everything here is for information only. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Do your own research and consult a licensed professional before making decisions.
