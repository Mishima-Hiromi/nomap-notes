AI Mechatec (6227 JP): net income up 9.5x in one year

A 250-person Japanese equipment maker, read straight from its EDINET filings

A Japanese company grew its net income 9.5x in a single year.

It is not a household name. It has about 250 employees.

Here is what it does, and what changed, using the numbers in its own filings.

About this series: each post takes one listed Japanese company and reads its disclosures on EDINET, the Japanese regulator's filing system (Japan's counterpart to SEC EDGAR), where most documents are published only in Japanese.

What the company does

AI Mechatec (securities code 6227) makes manufacturing equipment for semiconductor packaging, and for OLED and LCD panels.

Company website: https://www.ai-mech.com/

1. Net income went from JPY 0.34bn to JPY 3.56bn

Net income was JPY 0.34bn in FY2025.

In FY2026 it was JPY 3.56bn. That is 9.5x.

The chart shows how sharply it broke out of the low level that ran through FY2024 (JPY 0.11bn).

Revenue also rose, from JPY 21.0bn to JPY 35.2bn.

Operating cash flow (cash generated by the core business) went from JPY 1.53bn to JPY 11.18bn.

So the profit did not stay on paper. It came in as cash.

Return on equity (ROE) rose from 3.1% in FY2025 to 28.1% in FY2026.

2. Two shareholders sit side by side at the top

The largest shareholders are Tokyo Ohka Kogyo and Optorun.

Each holds 17.56%, exactly the same stake.

In the FY2024 annual securities report, the other large holder was JUKI.

By FY2025, Optorun had taken its place.

Optorun (6235) makes optical thin-film coating equipment. It announced a capital and business alliance with AI Mechatec in February 2025.

On valuation, the price-to-book ratio (PBR) stood at 9.0x as of FY2026. Much of the profit recovery already seems to be reflected in the share price.

Closing

A 9.5x jump in profit is something you see maybe once every few years.

The same chart is also honest about the years before it, at JPY 0.11bn and JPY 0.34bn.

Looking at both the peaks and the troughs gives a clearer picture of the company.
