Daiei Kankyo (9336 JP): four straight dividend increases, up 76%

A Japanese waste-management company with a steady dividend and one dominant shareholder, read from its EDINET filings

2026-09-28 · 日本語版

Few people think of dividends when they hear "waste disposal company."

This one is worth a look anyway.

About this series: each post takes one listed Japanese company and reads its disclosures on EDINET, the Japanese regulator's filing system (Japan's counterpart to SEC EDGAR), where most documents are published only in Japanese.

What the company does

Daiei Kankyo Co., Ltd. (securities code 9336, Tokyo Stock Exchange Prime Market) is an environmental services company. It runs industrial-waste landfill sites, incineration, and recycling as one integrated business.

Company website: https://www.daiei-kankyo.co.jp

1. The dividend went from JPY 30 to JPY 53 per share

The dividend per share was JPY 30 in FY2022.

The company then raised it four years in a row, reaching JPY 53 in FY2026. That is an increase of about 76% over four years.

Each year's increase fell between 10% and 23%. There was no year in which the dividend stood still.

2. One shareholder holds 61.49%

The other notable feature is the shareholder base.

The largest shareholder, Wing Twa Co., Ltd., holds 61.49% of the shares outstanding (FY2026). That is a high concentration for a listed company: in practice, a single shareholder can steer the direction of the business.

Why it matters for global investors

The dividend history and the shareholder table above both come from Daiei Kankyo's annual securities report, which is filed on EDINET in Japanese. A single shareholder with a majority stake means outside shareholders have limited influence on decisions, so the dividend track record is one of the few public signals of how the company treats them.

Wrapping up

A steady record of dividend increases, combined with one shareholder who controls the majority of the shares: those are the two facts the filings show most clearly.
