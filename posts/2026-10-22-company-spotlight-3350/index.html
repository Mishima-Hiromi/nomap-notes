Metaplanet (3350 JP): revenue up 8.4x, and a JPY 95.05bn net loss

A bitcoin treasury company on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Standard Market, read straight from its EDINET filings

A company that accumulates bitcoin as a corporate reserve asset is listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Standard Market.

Metaplanet (3350) increased revenue 8.4x in FY2025/12, the year ended December 2025, while posting a net loss of JPY 95.05bn.

Here is what the company does, and what changed, using the numbers in its own filings.

About this series: each post takes one listed Japanese company and reads its disclosures on EDINET, the Japanese regulator's filing system (Japan's counterpart to SEC EDGAR), where most documents are published only in Japanese.

What the company does

Metaplanet Inc. (securities code 3350, Tokyo Stock Exchange Standard Market) is a "bitcoin treasury company" that accumulates bitcoin as a corporate reserve asset.

In April 2024 it adopted a "Bitcoin Standard" structure, the second case in the world after Strategy, which is listed on Nasdaq in the United States.

Its consolidated subsidiary Wen Tokyo also runs a hotel business.

Company website: https://metaplanet.jp/

Business model: where the money comes from

Revenue for FY2025/12 was JPY 8.91bn, and 95.1% of it came from the bitcoin-related business.

Operating profit in that segment was JPY 7.19bn, for an operating margin of 84.9%.

The hotel business had revenue of JPY 0.44bn and operating profit of JPY 0.17bn, or 4.9% of revenue.

The two pillars are the treasury business, which holds bitcoin and grows it, and the bitcoin income business, which earns through options and other instruments using the bitcoin and cash it holds as collateral.

The income business began in the fourth quarter of FY2024/12 and lifted FY2025/12 revenue.

That income business does not buy goods and sell them. It earns through financial transactions collateralized by assets it holds.

No customer accounted for 10% or more of sales, and the annual securities report omits a list of major customers.

The money to buy bitcoin is raised through shares, preferred shares, and borrowings.

Proceeds from share issuance in 2025 were JPY 513.43bn. That figure includes JPY 21.2bn of Class B preferred shares (MERCURY) issued in December.

The company also set up a credit facility of up to USD 500m.

The value it provides

For investors, the value is being able to invest in bitcoin through a listed stock.

The company explains that gains on individuals' crypto asset transfers are expected to be subject to 20.315% separate self-assessment taxation from January 2028. As the tax treatment moves closer to that of listed securities, it expects participation by institutional investors and operating companies to widen.

The other value is increasing the amount of bitcoin held per share. The company discloses this per 1,000 shares.

At the end of 2025, fully diluted BTC per 1,000 shares was 0.0240486 BTC, 6.68x the 0.0035987 BTC of a year earlier.

BTC yield for full-year 2025 was 568%.

If the bitcoin it accumulates outpaces the dilution from share issuance, holdings per share rise.

Holdings at June 30, 2026 were 43,000 BTC, third among listed companies worldwide and first in Asia.

The company explains that it holds about 87% of the bitcoin held by Japanese listed companies.

Basic data

Here are Metaplanet's (3350) figures for FY2025/12. The same items appear each time.

  • Revenue: JPY 8.91bn
  • Operating profit: JPY 6.29bn
  • Operating margin: 70.6%
  • Net income: -JPY 95.05bn
  • ROE: -40.0%
  • Equity ratio: 90.7%
  • Dividend per share: JPY 0

Source: EDINET DB, annual securities report, FY2025/12.

Notable facts

Revenue rose from JPY 1.06bn in FY2024/12 to JPY 8.91bn in FY2025/12, 8.4x.

Operating profit also rose from JPY 0.35bn to JPY 6.29bn, about 18x.

Net income turned from JPY 4.44bn in FY2024/12 to a loss of JPY 95.05bn in FY2025/12.

That is because the company booked a bitcoin valuation loss of JPY 102.19bn in non-operating expenses.

The company explains that the valuation loss is an accounting adjustment reflecting quarter-end prices and does not directly affect cash flow.

Holdings rose from 1,762 BTC at the end of 2024 to 35,102 BTC at the end of 2025.

The major shareholders also changed.

In the FY2025/12 top ten, CLEARSTREAM BANKING S.A. newly entered with 8.66% and NATIONAL FINANCIAL SERVICES LLC with 8.38%.

CLEARSTREAM is a Luxembourg-based institution that handles securities custody and settlement.

INTERACTIVE BROKERS LLC, which was first the year before, fell from 9.82% to 3.75%.

Its share count, however, rose from 3.55m shares to 43.7m shares.

A stock split and a capital raise increased shares outstanding, which lowered the ratio.

Points to watch as an investor

The operating margin is high at 70.6%, while ROE is -40.0% and the equity ratio is 90.7%.

The main reason for the high equity ratio is the JPY 513.43bn of share issuance in 2025, and retained earnings stand at minus JPY 86.19bn.

The condition for growth is that holdings and market volatility keep translating into income revenue.

The company's forecast for FY2026/12 is revenue of JPY 16.0bn and operating profit of JPY 11.4bn. First-half results were revenue of JPY 4.94bn and operating profit of JPY 3.33bn.

The condition for a reversal is a fall in the bitcoin price.

In the first half of 2026 the company booked a bitcoin valuation loss of JPY 184.30bn, and the interim net loss was JPY 182.77bn.

The company explains that borrowings collateralized by bitcoin may require additional collateral or early repayment if the price falls.

What to confirm in the next earnings report is progress against the full-year forecast and whether BTC per 1,000 shares keeps rising.

Progress is 31% on revenue and 29% on operating profit, and the company explains that this is below its initial plan.

That is because in the second quarter enterprise value fell below the value of the bitcoin held, and under its policy the company postponed issuing common shares.

BTC per 1,000 shares at June 30, 2026 was 0.0263554 BTC, up 9.6% in the first half.

Why it matters for global investors

Metaplanet's disclosures sit on EDINET, and the underlying annual securities report is published in Japanese, so the headline numbers are easy to misread from outside Japan. The shareholder register is a useful cross-check: Clearstream Banking S.A. (8.66%), which the filing describes as a Luxembourg-based securities custody and settlement institution, newly entered the top ten alongside National Financial Services LLC (8.38%). Interactive Brokers' stake fell from 9.82% to 3.75% even though its share count rose, because a split and a capital raise expanded the number of shares outstanding. The company also cites a Japan-specific tax premise, the expected 20.315% separate taxation on individuals' crypto asset gains from January 2028, as a reason institutional participation could widen.

Closing

An 8.4x revenue figure on its own makes the company look like a straight-line growth story.

In reality, however, the fundraising to accumulate bitcoin and the accounting gains and losses from its price moves are intertwined.

Separating operating earnings from the valuation of the assets it holds makes the company's current shape easier to grasp.
